Eight supplier PDFs are waiting in Invoices/incoming, which is open in the
file manager. Our payables ledger, Finance/Payables.ods, is open in
LibreOffice Calc behind it.

Enter every invoice that is not already in the ledger as a new row at the
bottom: invoice number, supplier, invoice date and amount, in the same style
as the rows already there. Save the ledger. Then move the invoices you have
dealt with into Invoices/entered, which is where the ones already entered
live.

Credit notes are not invoices: leave them in incoming. An invoice goes into
the ledger once, however many times it was sent.
