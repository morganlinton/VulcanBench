Three things are open: Relay Desk, our support tracker, in the browser at
http://127.0.0.1:8000/; the Q1 ledger (Finance/Q1-ledger.ods) in LibreOffice
Calc; and Finance/discrepancies.txt, empty, in the text editor.

Every invoice in the ledger should have one Billing ticket in Relay Desk with
the same amount, and every Billing ticket should have one ledger row. Find
every invoice where they disagree: an amount that differs, an invoice that is
in one place but not the other, or an invoice entered twice. Write each one
on its own line in discrepancies.txt, with the invoice number and what is
wrong, and save the file. Change nothing in the ledger or in Relay Desk.
